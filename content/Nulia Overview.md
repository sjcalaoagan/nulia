Nulia is an earth-like planet comprising of one major [[Abrica|continent]] surrounded by a vast ocean with small landforms scattered throughout. It orbits its star (Sola) once every 300 days, and each day in Nulia is equal to 30 hours on Earth, split between 18 hours of day and 12 hours of night. 

Inhabitants of Nulia are known as Nulians.
