There are 4 seasons in a nulian year, each one lasting approximately 2.5 nulian months or 75 days.

## Newlia
This marks the beginning of a nulian year. Temperatures during this time are at its coldest, giving snow to the northern regions.

## Waxia
After newlia ends, waxia thaws out the cold, making flowers bloom, and wildlife active again. Rainfall is also frequently observed during waxia.

## Fulia
The warmest season of the nulian year. Weather during this season is at its calmest, with almost no rainfall nor natural phenomenon.

## Wania
Wania slowly transitions the year back to newlia, with temperatures steadily dropping and leaves falling.
