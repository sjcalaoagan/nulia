## Nollism

Nollists believe that originally, Nulia was a purely ocean world with the only life being aquatic animals and [[Carithians]]. But after being defeated by a God that betrayed her, Goddess Nolla's body crashed into Nulia and perished. Her body served as fertilizer, while her clothes morphed into land, giving the [[Abrica|Abrican]] continent its unique shape today.

Due to the land being Nolla herself, believers of Nollism are known to be caretakers of the land and are very grateful for the dirt they step on. Resourcefulness and "Cycle" are the main practices of Nollism. A devout follower would not be wasteful of any natural resources, and what they take from the land must be returned. Due to this, burying the dead is the norm.

Around 44% of nulians practice Nollism.

## Azanamity

Azanamity is a religion mainly practiced in the Azanami Theocracy. Here, they believe that everything is already pre-planned; written in advance. It is said that to prevent the universe from being too chaotic, leading to universal anarchy, Azanami spent an eternity writing everything that will happen in the universe, down to the last ant's actions. 

Azanamists live a conservative lifestyle, as trying to be "chaotic" is like going against the writing. This is partly why Azanami's culture and architecture seems frozen in time. Serenity and flow are the main practices of Azanamity.

## Solonism

Solonists believe that Gods are actually nulians' enemies, as they left nulians to fend for themselves during the Hadal era. Due to this, believers of Solonists heavily rely on logic to explain phenomenons in Nulia. Instead of believing in a God, Solonists take it upon themselves to discover the truths of Nulia, which is why states dominated by Solonism often have more advanced technology than the others.

Solonists practice logicality and curiosity. Solonists are also often more independent.
