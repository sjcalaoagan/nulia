# Overview
Yana Zhurova was a prodigal alchemist teacher alongside [[Elva Bezalgette]] from the [[Republic of Mishka]] before she retired 6 years ago due to wanting more free time and the lack of students. She's extremely energetic and experimental, which often led to failed experiments, causing commotion in [[Kassandre Ruminity Academy]]. Because of this, her and Elva often argue about the correct ways to go about [[Alchemy]]. She was more infamous than famous in the academy due to her experiments.
