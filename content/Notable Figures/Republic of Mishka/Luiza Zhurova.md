# Overview
Luiza Zhurova is the eldest daughter of [[Yana Zhurova]] and [[Maxim Zhurova]]. She is infamous in [[Kassandre Ruminity Academy]] for being a delinquent. Due to being taught by her father [[Maxim Zhurova]], she is extremely proficient in physical and [[ruminity]] combat.
