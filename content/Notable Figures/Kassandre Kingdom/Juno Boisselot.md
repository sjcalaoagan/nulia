# Overview
Juno is a jester whose clumsy jokes and awkward delivery often make her the laughing stock of [[Capital of Kassandre|Kassandre]]. Despite this, she remains cheerful, determined to spread joy. She often stations herself in the Kassandre Fountain and relies on donations to get by. She's the breadwinner in her family in the outskirts of Kassandre, but her income could barely support herself, let alone her sick parents.

## Interview

### “You perform at the Kassandre Fountain almost every day. What made you decide that being a jester was the life you wanted?”
I like seeing people happy! That's really the only reason, eheheh...
### “Your jokes don't always get the reaction you hope for. How do you feel when people laugh at you instead of with you?”
It's nice when I can see them laugh, but I do wish they'd laugh at my jokes instead. I can't lie and say it doesn't hurt at times, especially when I'm doing my best.
### “You’re known for always trying to make people smile, even when you're struggling yourself. Why is making others happy so important to you?”
I always believe that this good deeds of mine would be rewarded. Even if it didn't, a happier world is a better world, no?
### “You’re the main provider for your family despite being only eighteen. What’s it like carrying that responsibility?”
It's... tough. Really tough. But I can't give up! After all, I promised them we'd live in the upper class no matter what!
### “Your parents are sick, and your earnings barely cover your needs. Do you ever worry that you won’t be able to take care of them?”
I'm doing the best that I can, but sometimes it really isn't enough. They always say I don't have to try so hard, but that hurts me more...
### “You’re a devoted Nollist. Has your faith ever helped you through a particularly difficult day?”
Plenty of times! Whenever I'm living a rough day, I know that [[Abrican Religions|Nolla]] has my back.
### “You’re actually a very talented singer, but you rarely let anyone hear you. Why are you so afraid of showing people that side of yourself?”
E-eh? A-am I really that good at singing? A-and where did you even hear that from?!
### “You seem to admire Queen Viviane quite a lot. What does it mean to you when she stops to watch your performances?”
To see the queen herself stop by to watch me perform... It's nervewracking but it just feels me with so much joy to be able to attract such a person!
### “Felix Alarie is your closest friend. What makes him someone you feel comfortable being yourself around?”
He's like my crutch. When I fall down, he supports me back up. He's kind, nice, dependable, and I would've gave up a long time ago had he not been with me... O-oh dear! I've rambled on again... auu...
### “If you suddenly had enough money that you never had to perform for donations again, what would you do with your life?”
I'd probably still be a jester. I know I'm bad at it, but there's something freeing and satisfying about such a job.
