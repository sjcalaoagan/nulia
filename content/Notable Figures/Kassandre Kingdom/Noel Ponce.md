# Overview

Noel Ponce is currently a student being mentored by [[Elva Bezalgette]] after graduating from the standard curriculum at [[Kassandre Ruminity Academy]]. He chose alchemy due to his past of not having access to any healing [[Potions|potions]] that ultimately killed his parents from sickness. He's timid and quite inept at alchemy, but he doesn't give up.
