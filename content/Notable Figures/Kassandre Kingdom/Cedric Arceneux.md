# Overview
Cedric Arceneux was one of the smartest and gifted Rumis of [[Abrica]]. Unfortunately, due to his search for power, he caused a series of events that led to him being a criminal and exiled from [[Kassandre Palace]]. He was also the reason why the [[Five Families|Arceneux]] family almost lost its standing as one of the Five Families.
