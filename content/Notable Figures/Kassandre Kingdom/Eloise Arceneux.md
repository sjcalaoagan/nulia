# Overview
Eloise Arceneux is the current head of the [[Five Families|Arceneux]] family. Due to her parents' sudden passing due to murder by the [[Puristes de la Rumie]] en route to [[Montaumart]], she became the head at an exceptionally young age (17), making her the youngest among the Five Family heads. Nevertheless, her family genes made her competent in managing [[Kassandre Kingdom]]'s education, albeit she's perpetually tired and crazy from studying. She's also [[Kassandre Ruminity Academy]]'s headmaster.
