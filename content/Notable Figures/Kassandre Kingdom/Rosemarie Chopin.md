# Overview
Rosemarie Chopin serves as the head maid in the [[Kassandre Palace]]. Her family, the Chopins, has been known to serve the [[Kassandre Family]] for generations. Rosemarie lived in the servant quarters of the Kassandre Palace since she was a baby, being taught by her father the duties of a headmaid, until she took his position after he retired of old age.
