# Overview
Antoinette Yonamine was formerly one of the royal knights at [[Kassandre Palace]]. She was the youngest to achieve a title at 19 years old, but after being disillusioned by the life, she retired at 22 and became a teacher at a dojo in her hometown, [[Bannet]]. Her parents were originally from [[Azanami Theocracy]], but migrated to [[Bannet]] in 844 Pristine Era due to her father's job.

## Trivia
- Antoinette began her hate for the nobility after she became a Royal Knight. She saw how egotistical they were, and hated how they talk down on the commonfolk
- Even though she became a Royal Knight, she only accepted the position so she could make her parents live a comfortable life. However, after they reassured her that they want her to choose her own path, she retired, and became a teacher in [[Bannet]].
- Her father works at a [[Ruminite]] shaping factory.
- Antoinette's rapier stance is incredibly defensive, which wore her opponents down before she takes any action.
- Whenever she visits the capital, she and [[Jean Blanc]] often have a sword duel.
