# Overview
Elva Bezalgette is [[Kassandre Ruminity Academy]]'s infamous alchemy teacher. Despite being the least chosen mentor, she was still known to be extremely impatient and having unrealistically high expectation for her students, leading those who did choose her to quickly quit. She's also incredibly defensive of alchemy even though [[Kassandre Kingdom|Kassandre]] considers it a dying form of ruminity and inferior to other forms of ruminity. Currently only mentors [[Noel Ponce]].

## Interview

### "Why did you become an alchemist?"
I love making [[Potions|potions]]. Isn't that reason enough?

### "Why did you decide to take on an apprentice?"
Those damn higher ups forced me to. Other than that, as much as I hate mentoring, I want alchemy to at least be remembered.

### "What's the biggest mistake you've ever made in your research?"
A part of [[Poinnet]] still has high concentrations of mana because of my failed experiment in perpetual mana. It's partly why I'm in the outskirts.

### "If your apprentice wanted to leave tomorrow, what would you say?"
Why should I care? I only use my words for the competent.

### "What kind of student disappoints you the most?"
I despise those who think they're better than everyone become reckless. Hm? Why are you making that face? It's irritating me.
