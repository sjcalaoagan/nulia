# Overview
Clementine Leclair is the right hand woman of [[Sacha Constantin]], and she acts as [[Puristes de la Rumie]]'s brain. She is the one that organizes hits, sabotages, and invasions, ensuring each operation runs smoothly. Despite being the brain and an absence of a left arm, she is still formidable at using her greatsword at her back. She joined the faction at 16 when drunk [[Rumis]] from [[Republic of Mishka]] got angry and killed both of her parents at the tavern they own.

## Interview

### “You were only sixteen when your parents were killed. Do you remember what you felt when you first decided to join the Puristes de la Rumie?”
I felt a great sense of revenge. Anyone would if you see your parents die in front of you.

### “You organize assassinations, sabotage, and invasions, yet you’re known to be unusually kind toward Kassandrian commoners. How do you reconcile those two sides of yourself?”
I love the civilians of this kingdom, which is why I have to kill those who threaten their safety. Despicable ones they are, giving power to the enemies.

### “You openly despise Rumis from outside Kassandre. Do you hate them as individuals, or what they represent to you?”
I hate how they abuse the power given to them by our kingdom. I'm sure each one is as rotten as those who killed my parents.

### “You lost your left arm because of the violence surrounding your parents’ deaths. Has becoming physically disabled ever made you feel vulnerable?”
Do you see me trembling in fear? I could lose my entire head and I would still manage to kill someone.

### “You talk to your greatsword as if it were alive. What does it mean to you?”
It's my family. They've been with me through thick and thin.

### “You’ve crossed paths with Royal Knights and even oppose Queen Viviane Kassandre herself. Do you see them as enemies, or as people who simply chose the wrong side?”
They're people who are blind to see that our kingdom is being taken advantage of. I want to stop them before they kill anymore with their naivety.

### “If the Puristes de la Rumie succeeded in achieving everything you want, what would Kassandre look like afterward?”
A nation feared by the outside, and that's perfectly fine. It keeps them from going in.

### “If you could speak to the sixteen-year-old Clementine who had just lost her parents, what would you tell her?”
I'd tell her to do the same thing I did, except dodge to the right so I'd still have my arm.
