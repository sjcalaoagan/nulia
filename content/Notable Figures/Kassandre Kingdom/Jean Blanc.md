# Overview
Jean Blanc is the current head in the [[Five Families|Blanc]] family. He's an incredibly experienced [[Rumis|Rumi]] warrior, and he's also the one who trained [[Antoinette Yonamine]] instead of the usual [[Five Families|Laurens Family]] that trains the royal knights. He's straightforward, and doesn't like to mince words, and often clashes with [[Viviane Kassandre]]'s peaceful ideals.
