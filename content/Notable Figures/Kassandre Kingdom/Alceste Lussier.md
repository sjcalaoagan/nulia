# Overview

Alceste Lussier is one of the royal guards stationed at [[Kassandre Palace]]. He currently serves as the monarch's right-hand man, often accompanying her. He is very devoted to his duty, and is quite overprotective over [[Viviane Kassandre]], especially with how much she goes outside.
