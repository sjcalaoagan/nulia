# Overview

Viviane Kassandre is the current monarch of the [[Kassandre Kingdom|Kingdom of Kassandre]]. She's been ruling since 865, when she was only 26 years old. She was known to be benevolent and a major step-up from her father, Jacque Kassandre. She has imposed laws that benefit the more rural towns of Kassandre, and her vision is a more equal society. She's also responsible for maintaining the main core of [[Crescenda]]

## Interview

###  "You've been queen since you were twenty-six. Looking back, what was the hardest lesson you had to learn during your first year on the throne?"
Hmm, I would say the responsibilites? I never knew my father would be doing so much work. When he passed, I was thrusted straight into the throne without any experience besides my father's sparse teachings.

### "People often describe you as a queen who walks among her people instead of ruling from afar. Why is speaking directly with ordinary citizens so important to you?"
The citizens are the kingdom, is it not? If I get to know them better, I also get to know the kingdom better. I get to see what problems they may have that I can't see from my throne.

### "Your Majesty, critics say your kindness can be mistaken for weakness. How do you respond to those who believe a ruler should be harsher?"
Ahahaha, I understand why they may think that way. My father also said the same thing, but I believe treating everyone with kindness gives you more power also. A well-liked ruler will be listened to.

### "The heads of the Five Families hold immense influence over the kingdom. How do you balance their advice with your own judgement?"
Ah, it's more like they're my own brains. I compile each of their answers and merge them into one solution. I truly am grateful to their insights.

### You've often said you dislike war and conflict. If diplomacy failed and Kassandre faced invasion, what would be your greatest fear?
I fear of no longer seeing the smiles I've encountered in my time as a monarch, and even before that. I can not even think where my citizens would relocate should we face such tragedy, but I have great faith in the [[Five Families|Blancs]] to keep the kingdom from such a scenario.

### Many citizens have noticed that you carry a flower whenever you travel. Does it have a special meaning?
Ah, you've noticed as well? It doesn't particularly hold any meaning. It serves for my fingers to play with and calm me down at times. I make sure to give it to someone after my travels so that they, too, may also experience my journey.

### When the crown comes off and you're no longer attending court, who is Viviane Kassandre?
I'm simply another citizen of [[Kassandre Kingdom|Kassandre]]. I enjoy tea with [[Rosemarie Chopin|Rosemarie]], and I wander around the city just like the rest of you.

### Is there anyone who can still make the Queen of Kassandre lose her composure?
Cough! I-I don't know who- what you're pertaining to.
