The five families are the [[Kassandre Kingdom]]'s pseudo-rulers. They have the highest authority beside the monarch, and each family specializes in one role.

## House of Blanc
The Blanc Family specializes in the kingdom's defenses. Every member are extremely proficient [[Rumis]] and warriors. They oversee the nation's military, ensuring they're prepared for battle at any given moment. The current family head is [[Jean Blanc]].

## House of Cartier
The Cartier Family specializes in the nation's economy. They're the richest in Kassandre, and one of the richest in [[Abrica]]. Members of this family are trained in business, and also serve as diplomats for trade with other nations. The current family head is [[Ernest Cartier]].

## House of Arceneux
The Arceneux Family specializes in the nation's education. Each member is a scholar with large contributions to research. They overseer each school in Kassandre, including [[Kassandre Ruminity Academy]]. They're also the main accreditors for registering [[Rumis]]. The current family head is [[Eloise Arceneux]].

## House of Laurens
The Laurens Family specializes in the nation's governance. All members are trained in politics and law, and they serve as the nation's police and ministers. They're also the only family to have access to [[Crescenda]]. The current family head is [[Ava Laurens]].

## House of Saint-Pierre
The Saint-Pierre Family specializes in the nation's religion and culture. Members of this family oversee Kassandre's religion of [[Abrican Religions||Nollism]], as well as the various festivals. They're also the archivers of the kingdom, with their estate in [[Capital of Kassandre|Kassandre]] having historical documents and artifacts kept safe. The current family head is [[Anastasia Saint-Pierre]].
