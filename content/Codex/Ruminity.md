# Overview

Ruminity is one of the most influential forms of combat, defense, and utility in [[Abrica]]. Ruminity works through [[Ruminite]], which acts as ammo and an energy source. Ruminite is slotted into weapons or mechanisms to make it work. Ruminite can be shaped into different forms, which allows for other elements to be used. One of the most-widely used forms is the Triangle Ruminite, which powers most [[Abrica|Abrican]] infrastructure and households.

## Uses

### Combat
Square-shaped Ruminite can be slotted into staves and guns. The element it uses is based off of a weapon's catalyst, which is a glass container containing the element. When Ruminite comes into contact with the element, its energy matches it. This allows [[Rumis]] to shoot rocks or concentrated water.
### Defense
Square-shaped Ruminite may also be used to power barriers. A barrier's size and strength is dependent on the conduit, and the power consumed is also directly proportional to the size and strength. Other than specialized defense barriers, energy created from Square Ruminite may cancel each other out if both are of equal strength.

### Daily Life
Triangle-shaped Ruminite is commonly used in [[Abrica|Abrican]] households. They provide a small, stable power that enables Abricans to power their home appliances.
