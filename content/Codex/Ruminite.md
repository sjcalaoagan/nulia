# Overview

Ruminite is an integral component in [[Ruminity]]. It acts as the power for all Ruminity-related items such as Ruminite bulbs, weapons, and others. While Ruminite exists in all of [[Abrica]], it is rare and little in amount, with the exception of [[Kassandre Kingdom]], where 96% of Ruminite is extracted.

Ruminite's base form is useless, and requires shaping into other forms. Ruminite is initially chaotic in shape, but after shaping, its effect changes. Attempting to activate base Ruminite  results in a random effect, from low emissions, to big explosions.

For unknown reasons, planting Ruminite into [[Abrica]]'s land causes it to regenerate its power. The process is quite slow, however, and "charging" Ruminite cannot be used, as its energy is unstable during this time. Typically, a regular-sized Ruminite chunk has its power returned after 6 months.

## Usage

Shaped Ruminite is slotted into Ruminity-items, which contain wiring that directs its power throughout the device. For safety reasons, most Ruminity-items are equipped with a switch that can interrupt the flow of Ruminite's power, allowing the device to be safely turned on or off.

## Shapes

### Triangle
Triangle-shaped Ruminite acts as a battery source. It is the most popular shape, as it powers the majortiy of [[Abrica]]. One Triangle Ruminite can power an average Abrican home for 3 days. Some states of Abrica have companies which manage this power to reliably supply homes without much interruptions.

### Square
Square-shaped Ruminite acts as an ammo for guns and staves. Its depletion rate depends on the power of the vessel. Compared to Pentagon Ruminite, Square Ruminite is much more controlled, which is ideal for firing bursts of powerful energy.

### Pentagon
Pentagon-shaped Ruminite are highly unstable, often being used for explosives. When activated, it releases all of its energy at once. The production of Pentagon Ruminite is heavily regulated and monitored.
