The Royal Knight title of the [[Kassandre Kingdom]] is one of the highest positions a Kassandrian soldier could receive. Initially, a prospective knight is given a 3 year training before being official. Afterwards, they have the option to apply for the position of a Royal Knight. Many knights do, as there is no downsides to applying, but they multiple barriers before training even begins.

## Application Process

Firstly, the knight in question must already served for 5 years minimum. During this time, their record must be pristine and their personality up to the standards of the [[Five Families|Laurens]] Family. Should the knight have any case of disciplinary violations, recklessness, poor treatment of civilians, abuse of power, or poor judgement, their application is immediately rejected. During this time, the applicant or their commanding officer may be interviewed.

After they pass screening of the [[Five Families|Laurens]], their next hurdle is the physical test from the [[Five Families|Blanc]] Family. Here, their physical and ruminity prowess are tested with regulated duels. Their situation skills are also tested, aiming to see their judgement. They're also trained in disarming and escorting through roleplays.

Every 20th day of each month, the two families convene to discuss potential candidates. After a talk that usually lasts for 2 hours, they choose up to ~20 knights to become part of the Royal Army, giving them the title of the Royal Knight.

Despite the strict requirements, some knights can skip the application process entirely. Should a soldier commit extraordinary achievements with a great impact to the Kingdom will receive the title. Similarly, knights who have personally caught the attention of the heads of Laurens and Blanc may be nominated to become a Royal Knight, skipping the process entirely. One such case is [[Antoinette Yonamine]].

## Benefits

Despite the title, a royal knight's duty isn't dissimilar to that of a regular knight. Only this time, they're stationed at important estates like any of the [[Five Families]]' estates and the [[Kassandre Palace]]. Of course, their treatment is also extravagant, but that is usually up to the Family's hospitality. They're also sent to greater missions involving more dangerous matters. Lastly, they are given a home at [[Capital of Kassandre|Kassandre]] free of charge, and their family may also attend any royal gatherings. Their salary is ~175% that of a regular soldier.
