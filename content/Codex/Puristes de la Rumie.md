Puristes de la Rumie is a criminal group present in the [[Kassandre Kingdom|Kingdom of Kassandre]] who hates the idea of other countries having access to [[Ruminite]]. They believe that only the kingdom should be the only user of [[Ruminity]], as it was bestowed upon them first. They're also highly critical of any non-Kassandrians, and highly critical of the current government that allows it.
