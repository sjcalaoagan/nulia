# Overview
Lesorom is one of [[Republic of Mishka]]'s cities. It is known for its delicious cuisine, and is considered the food capital of Mishka. Due to having access to a port, it is also known to import ingredients from other states, making fusion cuisine a major part in Lersorom's economy.
