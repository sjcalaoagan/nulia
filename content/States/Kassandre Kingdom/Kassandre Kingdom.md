# Overview
Kassandre Kingdom resides in the southwest part of [[Abrica]]. The kingdom is known for being the greatest exporter of [[Rumis]] and potions. It's the current leader of ruminity research. It is one of the newer states in Abrica, only being founded at the beginning of the [[Wisdom era]].

---

- Area: 34,000 miles^2
- Location: Southwest Abrica

## Government
The Kingdom of Kassandre is a hereditary monarchy ruled by the [[Kassandre Family]]. Since the kingdom's founding during the [[Wisdom era]], every monarch has descended from the family. The current monarch is [[Viviane Kassandre]].

While the monarch has absolute power, the [[Five Families]] are usually the one giving advices and overseeing various fields such as military, economy, education, law, religion and culture. This not only divides the workload, but also prevents the monarch from having too much power.

### Monarch
- Title: Queen
- Current Ruler: [[Viviane Kassandre]]
- Residence: [[Royal Palace of Kassandre]]

### Succession
Should the current monarch pass away, the heir to the throne would be the eldest child unless overruled by the [[Five Families]] or willed by the current monarch. Should the current monarch not have any children, a member from any of the Five Families will be voted in and absorbed into the Kassandre lineage.

### Judicial System
Kassandre has a unique justice system called the [[Crescenda]]. Maintained by the [[Kassandre Family]], the artifact judges all proceedings, and its answer cannot be overruled.

---
## Economy
Being the leading figure in ruminity, Kassandre is the biggest exporter of ruminity-related produce like [[Rumis]] and potions. Similarly, due to being a powerhouse in ruminity, many students from other states go to [[Capital of Kassandre|Kassandre]] for its presitigious academy and to be licensed as an official [[Rumis|Rumi]]. This is the biggest source of revenue for the kingdom.
